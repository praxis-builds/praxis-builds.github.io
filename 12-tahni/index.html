    THE RULES

    A small board.
    A thoughtful game.

    1. Take your turn. Ivory starts. Each player has twelve pieces; the centre begins empty.
    2. Follow the lines. Move one point to an empty connected point. You can move forwards, backwards, or sideways. Diagonals work only where a line is drawn.
    3. Jump to capture. Jump one adjacent enemy piece and land on the empty point directly beyond it. The jumped piece leaves the board.
    4. Captures are optional. You may make a normal move instead. After a capture, you may jump again with that same piece or select “Finish turn”. You cannot step or switch pieces during a chain.
    5. Win the board. Capture every enemy piece, or leave your opponent without a legal move.
    6. Draw. Three occurrences of the same board and side to move after completed turns end the match.

    Captures and chain continuation are optional. A position is an automatic draw on its third occurrence at a completed-turn boundary with the same side to move and piece arrangement. Intermediate chain states do not count. Wins take precedence over a draw. Undo is available in local modes only.
